Privacy Policy — Storytecno Studio

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Storytecno Studio is an internal tool of the YouTube channel Storytecno. It produces videos, publishes them to that channel, and shows the channel's own performance metrics in a dashboard that runs locally on the channel owner's computer. The tool is not offered to the public.

Use of YouTube API Services

Storytecno Studio uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the application you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data is also handled in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Video uploads (youtube.upload scope): publish and schedule the videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails produced by the tool on the authorized channel.
  • Channel read access (youtube.readonly scope): subscriber count, views and public statistics of the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly scopes): daily views, watch time, retention, subscribers gained and estimated revenue of the channel.

No data about other users, viewers or channels is collected.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to build its performance dashboard.
  • Access tokens and metrics are stored only on the channel owner's local computer. There is no external server.
  • Data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties and is not used for advertising.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used.

Retention and deletion

Metrics obtained from YouTube API Services are refreshed every 6 hours and are never stored for more than 30 days; older records are deleted automatically. They can also be deleted at any time by removing the local database. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the tokens stop working and any stored data is deleted within 30 days.
